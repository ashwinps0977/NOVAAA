SALARY STRUCTURE POLICY

1. Salary is calculated on Cost To Company (CTC) basis.

2. Components of salary include:
   - Basic Pay (50% of CTC)
   - House Rent Allowance (HRA) (40% of Basic)
   - Special Allowance (Remaining amount)
   - Conveyance Allowance (Fixed ₹1600 per month)
   - Medical Allowance (₹1250 per month)

3. Statutory deductions:
   - Provident Fund (PF) = 12% of Basic Pay
   - Professional Tax = As per state government rules
   - Income Tax = As per Income Tax Act of India

4. Net Salary = Gross Salary - Total Deductions

5. Salary is credited on or before the 5th of every month.

6. Salary revisions are done annually based on:
   - Performance review
   - Company financial status
   - Market benchmarks

7. Interns receive consolidated salary without PF or tax deduction.

8. Salary details are confidential and accessible only to the employee, HR, and Admin.

9. Salary discrepancies must be reported within 7 working days.

10. Overtime is applicable only for approved roles and requires prior manager approval.
