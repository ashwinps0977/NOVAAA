BONUS AND INCENTIVE POLICY

1. Annual bonus eligibility:
   - Minimum 1 year of service
   - Performance rating of at least "Good"

2. Bonus amount is calculated as:
   - 5% to 20% of annual CTC based on performance

3. Performance categories:
   - Outstanding: 20%
   - Excellent: 15%
   - Good: 10%
   - Average: 5%
   - Poor: Not eligible

4. Festival bonus is paid during Diwali.

5. Spot bonuses may be awarded for exceptional contribution.

6. Sales team incentive is based on:
   - Target achievement
   - Revenue generated

7. Bonus is credited with salary or separately as per management decision.

8. Bonus is taxable as per Income Tax Act.

9. Employees under notice period are not eligible for bonus.

10. HR and Admin decide bonus distribution.

11. Bonus cannot be demanded as a right.

12. Bonus rules may change based on company performance.

13. Bonus history is maintained for each employee.
