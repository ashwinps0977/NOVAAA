PAYSLIP GENERATION RULES

1. Payslip is generated monthly for every active employee.

2. Payslip contains:
   - Employee Name
   - Employee ID
   - Department
   - Designation
   - Month and Year
   - Basic Pay
   - HRA
   - Special Allowance
   - Deductions (PF, Tax, Professional Tax)
   - Net Pay

3. Payslip format follows company payroll template.

4. Payslip is generated only after attendance verification.

5. Loss of Pay (LOP) is calculated based on:
   - Total working days
   - Unpaid leave days

6. Payslip is accessible through employee portal.

7. HR can regenerate payslip if correction is needed.

8. Digital signature of HR is included.

9. Payslip is password protected (DOB or Employee ID).

10. Payslip data is stored securely for audit purposes.

11. Payslips are retained for minimum 7 years.

12. Salary breakup shown in payslip must match salary policy.

13. Payslip generation date is between 1st and 5th of every month.

14. Payslip cannot be edited by employees.
