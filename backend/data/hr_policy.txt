HUMAN RESOURCE POLICY

1. Working hours are from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday.

2. Employees are entitled to:
   - 12 Casual Leaves per year
   - 12 Sick Leaves per year
   - 10 Paid Leaves per year

3. Probation period is 3 months for all new employees.

4. Attendance is mandatory and monitored via the HR portal.

5. Late login beyond 15 minutes will be marked as half day.

6. Employees must apply leave through the HR system.

7. Code of conduct:
   - Maintain professional behavior
   - No harassment or discrimination
   - Confidential company data must not be shared

8. Disciplinary actions:
   - Verbal warning
   - Written warning
   - Termination (for repeated violations)

9. Resignation policy:
   - Notice period: 30 days
   - Salary will be deducted for unserved notice period

10. HR is responsible for:
    - Recruitment
    - Payroll processing
    - Employee engagement
    - Policy enforcement

11. Performance appraisal is conducted every 12 months.

12. Promotions are based on:
    - Performance
    - Skills
    - Business requirements

13. Remote work is allowed only with manager approval.
